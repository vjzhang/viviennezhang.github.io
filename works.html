The Chameleon's Limit: Investigating "Persona Collapse" and Homogenization in Large Language Models (2026)

This paper investigates the phenomenon of persona collapse, a population-level homogenization in model-generated data points that fail to evenly occupy the human distribution. We propose a geometric framework to quantify population diversity of simulated personasa and systematically measure persona collapse. (Accepted at UserSim @ NeurIPS 2026)

Authors: Yunze Xiao†, Vivienne J. Zhang†, Chenghao Yang, Ningshan Ma, Weihao Xuan, Jen-tse Huang (†: equal contribution)

FELICITYBENCH: Pragmatic (In)felicity Sensitivity in LLMs (2026)

We introduce FELICITYBENCH, a benchmark for evaluating large language models' pragmatic competence with respect to felicity conditions, the contextual and social requirements that must be satisfied for a speech act to be successfully performed. Term paper for course Natural Language Processing.

Co-Authored with Surya Chinnappa.

Dajing Documentation Project (2025)

The project website hosts information collected during the first field trip to document the Dajing dialect in summer 2025. The goal of this project is to support the preservation and revitalisation of Dajing, an underdocumented Wu dialect spoken in Dajing Town, Xianxi Town and surrounding areas. The materials currently presented on this site are preliminary fieldwork results.

Website Meta-documentation

Morenthian - constructed language (2024)

Morenthian is a work-in-progress artlang developed for world-building purposes. It is an a priori language designed to be spoken by a group of humans in a fictional world. The description in the conlang document provides a rough sketch of the most current version of Morenthian.
